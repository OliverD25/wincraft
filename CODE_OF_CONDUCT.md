# Code of Conduct

WinCraft follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
a standard set of behaviour rules used by many open-source projects. The full
text is at that link. In short:

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, also with people who are new to Rust, to Windows
  programming, or to English.
- Talk about the code and the idea, never about the person.
- Accept that a review asks for changes; give reasons when you ask for them.
- Assume good intent, and say clearly when something bothers you.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, mocking, harassment, or unwanted attention of any kind.
- Discrimination for any reason: age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender,
  experience, nationality, religion, sexual orientation, or anything else.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, trolling, or other behaviour that keeps others from working.

## Where it applies

In this repository: issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and commit
messages, and anywhere someone speaks for WinCraft in public.

## Reporting a problem

Contact the maintainer, [@OliverD25](https://github.com/OliverD25), privately
through GitHub. Every report is read and kept confidential. The maintainer may
remove comments, close threads, or block people who break these rules, and
explains the decision to the people involved.

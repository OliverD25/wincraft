Update panel

While an update runs

Shows at the bottom right of the monitor under the mouse whenever the user starts an update: palette "Update WinCraft", About's Update now, or the "Update ready" row. Same look as the "Updated to" panel. One line per step. The bar fills to the percent while downloading, is full while restarting, and sweeps a third-long piece once every 1.4 s when the end is not known. A failure has no bar, holds 8 s, then fades. A click opens About.

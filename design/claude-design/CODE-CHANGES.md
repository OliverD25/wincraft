# UI changes made in code

Every change to a screen, layout, component, colour or on-screen wording that
did not come from a design pull gets an entry here, in the same commit. The
design session reads this file and brings the design in line, or says why not.

Status is `not yet in design` until a pull shows the design has it; then
`in design (pulled <date>)`.

| Date | What changed and why | Files | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-10-27 | Fluent palette on glass: the window corner radius is 8 px, not the Fluent 12 px. Windows clips a glass window to its own 8 px corners, so a 12 px painted corner left a strip of bare glass at each corner. Without glass (battery saver, transparency off) Fluent keeps 12 px. | `src/ui/palette/panel.rs` | not yet in design |
| 2026-10-27 | ShortcutDetector window built from `Shortcut Detector.dc.html` with these differences: the native Windows title bar (no W mark, no custom 40 px buttons), as every other WinCraft window has; the shared search box (36 px, 14 px text, with a Ctrl+F chip) instead of 32 px / 13 px; at the default 760 px width Refresh and Copy list wrap to a second toolbar line, aligned right; the hook note moves to its own line when it does not fit; App rows keep "(Windows: …)" in Meaning; a sorted column shows ↑ or ↓ after its name; "Copied N rows." shows for 3 s in place of the hook note. | `src/plugins/shortcut_detector/window.rs`, `rows.rs` | not yet in design |
| 2026-10-28 | QuickActions toggle rows built from `design_handoff_quickactions_toggles` with these differences. The eight tile colours are fixed per command, not theme tokens, so in light themes and High contrast the on tile is the same colour on a light panel; the design has no light-theme toggle row yet. The off tile fill is the theme's hover tint (text colour at 5 %, so it also shows on light panels) instead of white at 6 %, and its border is the theme's `border` token (#444141 in Classic dark, not #5c5858). The switch knob when on is the theme's window colour (#1c1a19 in Classic dark). The row keeps the theme's row height (44 px in Classic), not 48 px; the 28 px tile is centred on the 26 px icon slot so titles stay aligned with other rows. A toggle with a hotkey shows its keycaps before the switch. A row with no switch (no window, state unknown) shows the Enter pill when selected, like other rows. Until the state arrives (a few milliseconds after the palette opens) the line shows the command's own subtitle and the tile shows off. | `src/ui/palette/rows.rs`, `src/ui/widgets/toggle.rs`, `src/plugins/quick_actions/states.rs` | not yet in design |
| 2026-10-28 | WindowDeck: Shift and a digit opens the card ten higher (Shift+1 = card 11, Shift+0 = card 20), so cards 11-20 can be reached with one key press. The user chose Shift over a two-digit wait; Ctrl+digit stays the taskbar button. Cards 21 and higher use the arrow keys. When this desktop has more than ten cards, the footer shows one more hint chip after "1-9 Open by number": "Shift + 1-0  Cards 11-20". | `src/ui/arrange/strip.rs`, `window.rs`, `rows.rs`, `by_monitor/chrome.rs` | not yet in design |
| 2026-10-28 | About: the switch "Check for updates daily" now reads "Check for updates automatically", with the help "Asks GitHub every hour and tells you. Nothing installs until you click Update now." The check now runs every hour, about 30 s after a wake, and when the palette opens after 15 quiet minutes. The design's "Found by the daily check" becomes "Found by the update check". | `src/ui/settings/about.rs` | not yet in design |

ClaudeDeck

A new standalone WinCraft plugin (decided 2026-11-12 in a Q&A): a compact, vertical list of your Claude Code chats in its own small window. WindowDeck stays as it is. Classic style, with the colours and the Waiting / Working / Background / Idle wording of the page "Window Deck Claude Status" as the base. Rows never move by themselves; you drag them into an order. Picked by you on 2026-11-12: the shapes for the 9 status marks, with a setting "Status mark" that adds a short word next to each shape; and tree lines for forks.

11 chats in 3 groups and the "New" group, and 6 cold chats folded at the end; one family with 2 forks, one of them nested; every status at least once.

11. Homelab servers (decided 2026-11-12)

Each server from the setting is a group under the PC chats. A server icon replaces the square colour mark of a normal group, so a server never looks like a group you made; its round dot is green when it answers and grey when not. Its line shows whether it answers, CPU and RAM, and how many PC chats wait on it. Its chats are the Remote Control chats plus any chat active in the last 24 h. A PC chat blocked by a foreground ssh command gets the new status "Waits on <server>". A click on a server chat opens Windows Terminal with ssh, in that chat's folder.

Rules

  • Rows never move by themselves. A new chat goes to the end of its project's group; a fork goes under its chat.
  • No text under 12 px. Windows 11 fonts. Light and dark from the WinCraft theme.
  • A status always has its colour and its shape together.
  • No sound. The list, the tray count and the fade are the only signals. A chat that starts waiting makes a faded window solid again.
  • A click on a row brings that chat's window to the front. A chat with no open window opens nothing and says so in the hover panel.
  • Most urgent first, for folded groups and folded forks: Needs approval, Asks you, Done not seen, Working, Waits on a server, Background, Idle, Stopped, Failed, Unknown. (Failed sits low because nothing waits on it; say if it should be higher.)
  • Servers are read only while the window is open, and only read: WinCraft never changes anything on a server. Needs approval cannot be seen on a server, so such a chat shows Working there.

Rules for the developer

  • Fluent treatment: window acrylic (theme glass colour + backdrop blur 48, saturate 180%; fall back to solid elevated when transparency effects are off), 1 px rim, inset top highlight, radius 12. Inspector and Ask AI pill sit on a 3–45% white layer. Rows radius 6; selected row = warm tint + 3 × 16 accent pill at the left edge. Search field has a 2 px accent bottom line while focused. Icons sit on 28 px colour tiles (radius 7, white glyph 16): layout blue, file grey, save green, WindowDeck violet, calculator teal, AI amber, web sky; app tiles use the app's own icon.
  • Base layout, rows, inspector, settings gear and Ask AI pill as in 6a (Palette Main View.dc.html). WinCraft updates and the plugin store live in Settings only.
  • Scope prefixes: a prefix followed by a space at the start of the query becomes a chip (24 high, 1 px accent border, prefix in mono). > commands, w windows, a apps, = calculator, ? Ask AI; plugins may register their own. Backspace on an empty query removes the chip.
  • Enter hint: the selected row replaces its keycaps with an accent pill (24 high) holding the Enter verb and a ↵ keycap. The verb comes from the item's first action.
  • Inline answers: calculator and unit/currency conversion render as the first group while typing, value at 20 px tabular figures. Enter copies and closes; Ctrl+↵ pastes into the previously focused window.
  • Multi-select: Ctrl+Space or Shift+click marks window rows; a 16 px checkbox column appears after the first mark. The inspector lists the marked set and its actions apply to all. Esc clears marks before it closes the palette.
  • Learned ranking: per-query pick counts with a 14-day half-life. The top item gets a Best match group and an "often picked" badge with a clock icon. "Forget this pick" (Ctrl+Del) and a Settings switch turn it off.
  • Empty state: one line "No apps, windows or commands match." then a Try instead group with Ask AI (Enter) and Search the web (Ctrl+↵).
  • Window inspector: 96 px preview, then process and PID, monitor and desktop, memory, and a "Show on all desktops" switch, then actions.
  • Footer: keycap hints come from the selected item's actions (max 5, Esc Close always last), so they change with the selection.
  • Moving the palette. Press and hold on blank space and drag to move the window; it opens where it was left, per monitor. Double-click on blank space, or the command "Palette: Move back to the centre" (WinCraft group), puts it back at the default spot. Nothing is drawn for this: no hover or grab cursor.
  • Window edge. The coloured area around a window on this page is the desktop background, shown only to judge the look over a wallpaper. It is not part of WinCraft. The window ends at its 1 px border and rounded corners. Nothing outside it belongs to WinCraft: no outer frame, no margin, no extra glass. The shadow is the system shadow that Windows draws.
  • Acrylic. The acrylic fills only the window itself, edge to edge. The window is exactly the panel size, with Windows' own rounded corners and shadow. There is no transparent margin around it and no caption buttons (minimise, maximise, close) on the palette, WindowDeck or QuickJump.

Rules for the developer

  • List. An ordered list of up to ten windows, numbered 1–9 then 0. Unpinning closes the gap: later windows move down one number. Same list on every desktop. The slot strip and fixed slots are gone; the chord tip replaces the strip.
  • Keys. See section 1. The pin shortcut (Win+Alt+Shift+Q) has no keys by default; when the user gives it keys, its chord tip lists 1–0, A, ` and U. Warm red tip texts (1.2 s): "Quick access is full", "Already number N", "Not in Quick access", "There is no window to pin". A change that offers Undo removes the tip at once and the notice takes its place.
  • Quick access window. As wide as the cards in a row: two at least, four at most (1300 px with four, 660 with two). Padding 18/20/22, header 20 semibold with a 14 px count "N of 10" and key hints at the right. Grid gap 28 × 20. Opens in the middle of the monitor under the mouse each time; with the setting off, where it was last left, kept inside the screen.
  • Card. 300 × 250, padding 8, 2 px border, radius 9. Picture area 280 × 175 in panel fill, radius 6. Title 18 px, second line 13 px text secondary: "<app> · Desktop N · <monitor>", or "<app> · not open". Keycap 30 under the card; the 10th reads "0". Selected: accent border, accent 10 % fill, accent keycap. Hovered: text 5 % fill. A grip icon shows right of the title on the selected or hovered card; cards can be dragged to reorder.
  • Pictures at true size. One scale for every live picture: the largest that fits every window in 280 × 175. A narrow window shows narrow, a wide one wide, so windows on a portrait or smaller monitor are easy to find. Each picture sits centred in the area with a 1 px text primary 12 % outline that hugs it. A card with no picture (not open) keeps the full box: dimmed panel fill with the app icon (48) at 45 % in the middle.
  • Empty state. The window at its two-card width. Two dashed card outlines with dashed keycaps 1 and 2 show where windows will go (Lucide plus 24 in text disabled). Under them "No windows yet" 18 px, the line "In any window, press Win ` then A to add it here as number 1." with accent keycaps, 14 px text secondary, a 13 px text disabled line on numbering, and the Secondary button "Add a window to Quick access…" (opens the picker).
  • Details (F2). 360 px panel on the right, panel fill, titled "Number N details". Label, App, Title contains with a live match line, "If it is not open, start <app>". Unpin (Del) and Done (↵).
  • Picker. "Add a window to Quick access…" from the palette and the empty state. Adds to the end; footer "↵ Add as number N". Windows already in the list carry a tag "number N".
  • Notices. The existing notice with Undo (8 s, Ctrl+Z): "Added X as number N", "Moved X from A to B", "Unpinned X (was number N)". X is the pin's label, else the window title, else the app.
  • Window hint. After a jump to a window on another monitor than the one in use, see Window hint. Off with the settings switch "Point to a window on another monitor".

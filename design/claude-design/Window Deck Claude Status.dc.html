WindowDeck for Claude: who waits, who works, how old

When the app in WindowDeck is the Claude desktop app, each card shows what its chat is doing now and how old it is. The cards stay exactly where you put them: WinCraft never sorts them by status (user's decision, 2026-11-11). To see the chats by status, use the counters in the toolbar, which light up cards in place, or the Board (B), a separate view that changes nothing. Other apps keep today's WindowDeck. The status comes from the Claude Code hooks on this PC; nothing leaves the PC (Principle 5). Game style stays light and inside the Windows 11 look (Principle 6): a turning ring while Claude works, cards that cool down with age, and a small score for today. Four card styles: Classic, the default, and three fun styles to study: Quest board, Chat pets and Claude crabs. Pick one with the switch below; in the app it is a setting.

4 · What the marks mean
Status chip, top left of the picture
Waiting 4 min
The chat asked you something: a question card, an approval, a question at the end of its reply, or a question in the next-step box. The card keeps its orange frame until you answer. Time = how long it has waited.
Working 2 min
Claude is working on your last message. The ring turns while it works. Time = how long this turn has run.
Idle
The turn ended and nothing waits for you.
Age, under the title: time since the chat's last message
Freshunder 1 hourthe picture as today
Cooling1 hour to 1 daypicture at 85 %
Cold1 to 3 dayspicture at 60 %, a light blue tint, the age in blue
Frozen3 days or morepicture at 40 %, the snowflake and the age in blue
An old chat is only shown. WinCraft never closes, archives or moves it (Principle 1).

Keys (Principle 4, Keyboard first)
S lights up the next status: Waiting, Working, Idle, Cold, then none. A click on a counter does the same. Esc clears the light first, then closes WindowDeck.
B opens the Board. Arrows move between rows and columns, Enter opens the chat, Esc goes back to WindowDeck as it was.
All other WindowDeck keys work as today: arrows select, digits open card N, Ctrl + arrows move, Ctrl + W closes, F2 renames.
The score for today (right end of the toolbar)
answered: questions you answered today. finished: chats whose turn ended with nothing waiting. answer time: the middle time a question waited for you today. The green ring fills as the waiting list empties; it is full when nothing waits.
It starts again at midnight and is kept only on this PC. No badges, no sounds, no pop-ups.

Each chat is a quest. Cards 3 and 7 say YOUR MOVE! and their bar drains as they wait; card 7 has waited longer, so its bar is shorter. The header shows today's level and XP.

Quest board rules
YOUR MOVE!The chat waits for you. The orange bar drains as the wait grows: full when it just asked, almost empty after about 45 minutes.
IN PROGRESSClaude works on your message. The striped bar moves while it works.
RESTINGThe turn ended and nothing waits. The line shows when the chat last replied.
SLEEPING zZNo message for 1 day or more. The picture fades like in Classic.
Lv 3The chat's level: one level for every 5 turns in it. Long, busy chats have high levels.
XPToday's points, top right: 20 XP for each answered question, 10 for each finished turn, a bonus of 10 for an answer within 2 minutes. 500 XP is the next level. Starts again at midnight.

The counters with S, the Board with B, and every other WindowDeck key work as in Classic; only the cards and the header change. Waiting cards keep the orange frame in every style, so orange always means 'wants you'.

Each chat has a small crab that moves. Crabs 3 and 7 wave their claws at you, the working ones run from side to side, old chats sleep or sit in ice. The header counts them.

Claude crabs: one small animated crab per chat. Its colour is its status, never Claude's own orange: orange stays only for "wants you" (user decision, 2026-11-05).
Orange, waves both claws, a "!" above it: the chat asked you something.
Blue, runs from side to side with moving legs: Claude is working.
Green, breathes slowly and smiles: the turn ended, nothing waits.
Faded blue, eyes closed, a "z" floats up: no message for 1 day or more.
In a block of ice, shivers now and then: no message for 3 days or more.
The crabs move only while WindowDeck is open. When Windows has animations switched off (Settings › Accessibility › Visual effects › Animation effects), they stand still.

The counters with S, the Board with B, and every other WindowDeck key work as in Classic; only the cards and the header change. Waiting cards keep the orange frame in every style, so orange always means 'wants you'.

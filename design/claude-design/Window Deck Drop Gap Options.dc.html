WindowDeck: a gap shows where a dragged window lands

Today a 2 px accent bar in the 8 px space between two cards marks the landing place. A window that wants attention has an orange ring in the same space. In the Terminal theme the accent (#f0a640) and the attention orange (#e88a4a) look almost the same, so the bar is lost next to an orange card. The user's decision (2026-11-10): open a gap instead, as Quick access does since 2026-11-08. The other cards slide apart (200 ms, ease-out) and leave an empty slot as big as the dragged card. On release the card slides into it; Esc slides everything back. The bar goes away. B is chosen (user, 2026-11-10): a faint fill and a 1 px solid neutral ring. A and C stay below for the record. The frames further down use B.

Rules for every option

The slot is never orange, never the accent and never dashed. Orange means a window wants attention; a dashed outline means a ghost (a window on another monitor). The slot has the dragged card's width and height. The dragged card lifts: it is drawn above the others with the selected look (2 px ring in text_primary at 85 %, fill text_primary at 10 %), palette_shadow 8 px down with a 24 px blur, at 103 % size, and follows the mouse. Its old place keeps no faded copy any more: the gap is the only landing mark. Cards keep their orange rings and number chips while they slide.

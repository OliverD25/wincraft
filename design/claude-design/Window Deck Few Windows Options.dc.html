WindowDeck: a monitor with only a few windows

Two options for the cards when a monitor column has few windows and a lot of empty width. Everything else on the panel stays where it is: the app box, By monitor and Flat, Hints, the desktop line, the monitor line, the number chips and the selected look (the neutral ring of 2026-11-05). One change for all options: on a monitor that is not the main one, the strip has no toolbar (no app list, no By monitor and Flat switch, no Ctrl + H Hints), so the pictures get its height. The text is as short as in the compact popup: the app name with the arrow that shows other desktops, and one hint line. The "This desktop" line and the monitor line ("Right 2560 × 2880 3") are gone, because this strip only ever shows this desktop of this one monitor. A new setting, off by default, brings the toolbar back there (user's decision, 2026-11-07; the setting is at the end of this page). Pick A, B or C.

When all cards of a monitor fit on one line, every picture grows by the same factor until the line fills the column, up to 240 px tall (2.5×). The cards keep their shapes and their sizes to each other, as the windows have on the screen. The window gets taller by the extra height; nothing else moves. With more cards than fit on one line, the view is as today.

When all cards of a monitor fit on one line, they share the column width equally, up to 360 px a card. Each picture grows to 160 px tall and sits in the middle of its tile on the panel colour, in a frame that hugs it (as on Quick access cards). The title gets a full line. The window gets about 64 px taller. With more cards than fit on one line, the view is as today.

The cards of the compact popup (WindowDeck with one or two windows), used here when all cards of a monitor fit on one line. The number and the title sit on top, on their own line, so the title is never covered by the picture. Below it, the picture is on a dark box and fills the card width, its height from the window shape, from 90 to 240 px as in the compact popup. Cards share the width equally, up to 280 px a card. The selected card has the neutral ring on the raised colour. The window gets about 160 px taller. With more cards than fit on one line, the view is as today.

The new setting · Settings › WindowDeck
The app list and the By monitor and Flat switch, in the strip that opens from the taskbar of a monitor that is not the main one. Off gives the pictures more room.

Off by default. On the main monitor, and in the strip opened with Win+Alt+A, the toolbar always shows, Ctrl + H Hints included. On another monitor the strip keeps the view picked last, and its keys work as before, Ctrl+H too.

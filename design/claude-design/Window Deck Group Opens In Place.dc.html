  • The smallest footprint: three windows take less than two card widths.
  • Good for big groups: 6 windows still fit near the stack.
  • Only the front window shows its picture and full name; the others show a strip. ← → brings one to the front.
  • New for WinCraft: cards that overlap do not appear anywhere else in WindowDeck.

Rules for every option

  • No card moves, changes size or wraps to another line. The WindowDeck window keeps its size.
  • The open group is drawn on a layer above the row: an opaque panel with the group frame, its header line and a shadow.
  • The other cards stay dimmed underneath and do not react to the mouse. Their numbers pause.
  • Esc, or a click outside the group, closes it. Everything is then exactly as before it opened.
  • The group stays inside the WindowDeck window. If it is wider than its monitor column, it may cover the next column.
  • A group too wide for the whole window uses smaller cards, never text under 12 px.
  • While open, the hint bar shows: 1 – 3 Window in the group, Esc Close the group, Ctrl + G Take out of the group, Ctrl + W Close.

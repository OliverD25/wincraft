WindowDeck: groups of related windows

Related windows (here the three NewsHQ chats) fold into one stack card with one number, so WindowDeck stays short and the numbers go further. Pressing the stack's number opens the group in place; a second number opens one of its windows. User's decisions, 2026-11-11: you make a group yourself; it looks like a stack; its number opens it in place; groups are remembered by window names; a stack stands with most of its windows; one app per group; closing a stack asks first. Everything not named here stays as it is today.

Rules
  1. You make a group; WinCraft never groups windows by itself (Principle 1). Drag a card onto another card, or pick cards with Ctrl + click and press Ctrl + G. The name box suggests the start the window names share.
  2. A closed group is one stack card with one number. Its picture is the window you used last in the group. The count chip says how many windows it holds.
  3. The group's number (or Enter on the stack, or a click) opens it in place: its cards slide out inside a light frame, the other cards dim, and the group's windows are numbered 1, 2, 3 while it is open. So 6 then 2 opens the second window. Esc closes the group; Esc again closes WindowDeck.
  4. The numbers after a stack move up: a group of 3 frees two numbers for other windows.
  5. An orange frame (a window that wants you) shows on the stack too, when any window inside it wants attention, and on that window's card when the group is open.
  6. The stack stands on the monitor that holds most of its windows. A window on another monitor shows as a dashed ghost card inside the open group; its own monitor column shows it dimmed while the group is open.
  7. One app per group for now: a group lives in one app's list (the app box at the top left).
  8. Groups are remembered by the names of their windows, the way WindowNamer remembers names. A window that opens again with the same name joins its group by itself. A group with one window left shows as a normal card with a small stack mark; a group with none waits until a window comes back.
  9. Ctrl + W or a middle click on a stack asks first: "Close 3 windows in NewsHQ?". On a window card inside an open group they close that one window, as today.
  10. Ctrl + G on a window inside an open group takes it out of the group. Ctrl + G on a stack ungroups all of it. F2 on a stack renames the group.
  11. The Flat view shows the same stacks. The compact popup on other monitors shows a stack as a normal card with the count chip, and opens it the same way.

Show where the window went

When a jump brings up a window on a monitor other than the one the user was using, two click-through panels show together. A glow around the window marks it where it is. A pill on the monitor the user was looking at names the window and points to it. Neither takes focus or the mouse.

Rules for the developer

  • When. After a QuickJump jump to a window on another monitor than the one the user was using (the monitor with the focused window, else the mouse). Not for a jump on the same monitor. Off with QuickJump's switch "Point to a window on another monitor" (on by default).
  • Glow. A click-through panel over the window: a 3 px line in accent just outside the window's visible frame (DWM frame bounds), following its 8 px corners, with a 12 px soft outer glow in accent (55 % next to the line, fading to nothing). Maximised: along the inside of the monitor's work area, square corners.
  • Pill. On the monitor the user was using, 16 px inside the edge that faces the other monitor, in line with the window's middle, kept inside the work area. Notice panel look: elevated fill at 96 %, 1 px rim, window radius, the shared panel shadow; 40 high, 16 px side padding. "WinCraft" 12 px text disabled, 10 px gap, the name 14 px text primary, " · Right monitor" in text secondary, the arrow 14 px semibold accent 6 px away. The arrow points toward the window: ← first for a monitor on the left; → ↑ ↓ last otherwise. Name: the pin's label, else the window title, else the app; cut with "…" at 40 characters and 420 px.
  • Monitor words. Left, Centre, Right by the x of the work area; Upper and Lower for stacked monitors ("Upper monitor ↑").
  • Timing. Both fade in over 120 ms and out over 300 ms. Glow holds 400 ms, pill holds 1.2 s. With Windows "Animation effects" off, both show without fades for their hold time.
  • Text. Nothing below 12 px. Colours come from the theme, so light themes use their own accent and panel.

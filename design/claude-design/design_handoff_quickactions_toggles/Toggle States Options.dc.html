Each toggle is shown three ways, using the states read from your PC. The left column is the row as it looks now; the right column is the same row after Enter. You can pick a different option per toggle, for example 6c and 1b.

a · Switch. A switch at the right of the row. b · State icon. The icon tile changes shape and colour. c · Next action. The title says what Enter will do; a pill says the state now.
Chosen b + a for every toggle, as on your PC now

The icon from b shows the state at a glance; the switch from a confirms it at the edge, where every toggle lines up. The title never changes, so typing "mic" or "dark" always finds the same row.

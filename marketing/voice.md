# Voice

Status: draft
Updated: 2026-11-15

- Tone: plain, honest, first person ("I built this because..."), no hype.
- Audience: Windows 11 users who live on the keyboard, and people who run
  several Claude Code chats at once. They know their tools; they do not
  want a sales pitch.
- Simple English: one idea per sentence, about 15-20 words. The author is
  not a native speaker, and many readers are not either.
- Say what the user gains, then how: "never miss a chat that waits for
  you", then "a status mark on every chat".
- Words to use: free, open source, one small file, keyboard first, local,
  your own order, at a glance.
- Words to avoid: "revolutionary", "seamless", "blazing fast",
  "game-changer", "effortless", "best", "zero bloat", any number without a
  source.
- Name WinCraft as the app and each tool (ClaudeDeck, WindowDeck) by its
  own name.
